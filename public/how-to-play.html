How to play

A quick guide to every game on Party Games.

What is Party Games?

Party Games is a free, browser-based collection of 23 party games built for groups hanging out in the same room. One person creates a room and gets a short room code; everyone else opens the site on their own phone, types the code, and joins instantly. There's nothing to download and no account to create — the game screen lives on a TV, laptop, or just gets shared by looking at one phone together, while everyone's own phone becomes their personal controller for answering questions, drawing, voting, and buzzing in.

Rounds are short (most games run 15-60 seconds per turn), scoring is visible after every round, and a full game usually wraps up in 5-15 minutes — built for playing several back to back rather than one long session.

Getting started

One player taps "Create Room," picks a game and a display name, and gets handed a 4-letter room code. Everyone else taps "Join Room," enters that code and their own name, and the game begins once the host starts it. A host can change games, adjust settings like round length, or add a join password at any point before starting.

The games

🤳 Heads Up!

One player holds their phone to their forehead (or has someone else hold it up) so everyone but them can see the secret word on screen. The rest of the group gives clues out loud while the clock runs, and the player guesses as many words as they can before time's up.

😂 Prompt Battle

Everyone gets the same silly prompt — a caption, a fake headline, a "finish this sentence" — and writes their own answer in secret. All the answers get shown anonymously, and the group votes for the funniest one.

💣 Word Bomb

Players take turns naming a real word containing a given set of letters before a short timer runs out. Miss it, and you lose a life; run out of lives and you're out.

🧠 Trivia Blitz

Classic multiple-choice trivia across a mix of topics. Everyone answers at the same time, and answering faster earns bonus points on top of just being right.

🤔 Would You Rather

Two impossible options, one choice. After everyone locks in, the room sees how the vote split — matching the majority earns points.

🔤 Category Countdown

A category and a letter appear — name something that fits both before time runs out. Unique answers score more than ones other players also thought of.

👉 Most Likely To

A prompt like "most likely to get lost with GPS on" appears, and everyone votes for the friend who fits best. Whoever gets the most votes wins the round — and the people who voted for them score too.

🧩 Emoji Decode

A movie, show, or phrase is translated into a short string of emoji. First to correctly guess what it's supposed to mean scores the points.

📊 Guess the Crowd

A real statistic — "what percent of people have never seen Star Wars," that sort of thing — and everyone guesses the number. Closest guess to the real answer takes the round.

🤥 Fib or Fact

Everyone writes a convincing lie to go alongside the real answer to a question. Then the group tries to pick out the actual truth from the pile of fibs — fool people with your lie, or catch everyone else's.

💀 Quiz or Die

Trivia with elimination: get a question wrong and you lose a life. Last player still standing wins the game outright.

🎨 Doodle Guess

One player gets a secret word and draws it live, right on their phone, while everyone else watches the drawing appear in real time and races to type the correct guess first.

🔀 Anagram Blitz

A word's letters are shuffled into a jumble — unscramble it back into the real word before the timer runs out.

🔍 Odd One Out

Four things are shown, and three of them share something in common. Spot the one that doesn't belong.

➗ Quick Math

Simple arithmetic, but under real time pressure — it's less about the math and more about who can do it fastest without freezing up.

🕵️ Two Truths and a Lie

Each player writes two true statements about themselves and one made-up one. Everyone else tries to guess which statement is the lie.

🙋 Never Have I Ever

A "never have I ever..." statement appears, and everyone answers honestly with have or haven't. Being in the minority — the less common answer — is what scores points.

🗝️ Riddle Me This

Classic riddles, answered against the clock. Straightforward, but surprisingly good at splitting a room between people who get it instantly and people who really don't.

🔡 Countdown Letters

Nine random letters appear, and everyone tries to build the longest real word they can out of them before time's up. Longer words score more.

🖌️ Draw It!

Unlike Doodle Guess, everyone draws the exact same secret word at the same time, privately. Once every drawing is in, the room votes on their favorite.

📅 Guess the Year

A real historical or pop-culture event is named, and everyone guesses what year it happened. Closest guess wins the round.

❓ True or False

A statement appears that's either true or completely made up — lock in your answer before the timer runs out.

🔢 Rank It

Four items need to be put in the correct order — biggest to smallest, oldest to newest, whatever the round asks for. Points are awarded per item you get in the right spot, with a bonus for getting all four exactly right.
